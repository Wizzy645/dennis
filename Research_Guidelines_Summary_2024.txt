Research Project Writing Guidelines 2024 version423 — Summary

1) What the report is
- A postgraduate computing report presents results of a research investigation:
  problem finding, data gathering, literature reviewing, methodology mapping, development/implementation, results interpreting, and documenting/summarizing conclusions and recommendations.
- The report can be a Thesis/Dissertation/Research Project, depending on degree level.

2) Core specifications
- Research Title:
  - Must encapsulate the essence of the work in the fewest words.
  - Maximum of 20 words.
- Paper quality:
  - Not less than 75g/m².
  - A4 paper size (8.27 in × 11.69 in).
- Margins:
  - Left margin: 1.25 inches.
  - Right, top, bottom margins: 1.0 inch each.
- Document format:
  - Consistent formatting throughout.
  - Times New Roman, font size 12 for most content.
  - Double line spacing for most of the document.
  - Abstract and references should use single-line spacing.

3) Headings and typography
- Main headings:
  - Times New Roman, size 14
  - center alignment
  - bold formatting
- Other headings:
  - Times New Roman, size 12
  - multi-level numbering system as per the sample.
- Language:
  - British English only (no American English variant).
- Paragraphing:
  - block paragraphing: separate paragraphs with a single line space (no indentation).
- Mathematical notations/equations:
  - clear and consistent style
  - all equations must be numbered.

4) Pagination
- Preliminary pages:
  - Roman numerals (i, ii, iii, iv …)
- Chapter One and beyond:
  - Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3 …)

5) Figures and tables (captions & numbering)
- Every figure/table/drawing must have a self-explanatory caption.
- Numbering must follow the chapter it belongs to (e.g., 1.2, 2.2, 3.2, 4.4 …).
- Caption style:
  - follow APA 7th edition style guidelines.
- Placement rules:
  - Tables and figures may be integrated in the body or placed after the references section.
  - If on separate pages, tables precede figures.
- Label placement:
  - Table number above the table (left margin, bold).
  - Figure number below the figure (left margin, bold).
  - Title located one double-space below the table/figure number.

6) Referencing and citations
- Referencing style:
  - APA 7th edition
- In-text citations:
  - include citations in-line in the body of the work
  - two types:
    - Parenthetical: (Author, Year)
    - Narrative: Author (Year) in the sentence
- Reference list/bibliography:
  - include a comprehensive list at the end of the document after Chapter Five.
- Source quality:
  - use reliable, verifiable sources (published articles, books, whitepapers).
  - avoid referencing web pages and non-peer-reviewed publications when possible.
- Encourage citation tools:
  - Zotero, Mendeley, Citeulike, BibMe, RefWorks, EndNote.

7) Reference count & recency requirements
- Minimum references:
  - PGD: at least 25 references
  - Masters: at least 35 references
  - PhD: not explicitly stated in the extracted portion, but follows thesis expectations.
- Recency:
  - > 70% should be within the preceding 5 years.

8) Report length requirements (by level)
- PGD: 10,000–20,000 words
- Masters: 15,000–30,000 words
- PhD: above 35,000 words

9) Recommended main body structure (Chapters)
- Abstract
- Chapter One: Introduction
- Chapter Two: Literature Review
- Chapter Three: System Design and Methodology / Research Methodology
- Chapter Four: Implementation and Testing / Results and Discussion
- Chapter Five: Discussion, Summary, Conclusion and Recommendation
- References
- Appendices (if any)

10) Standard organization sequence (front matter)
Typical order:
- Cover Page
- Title Page
- Declaration Page
- Certification Page
- Acknowledgment
- Table of Contents (reflecting only 1st, 2nd, and 3rd tier headings)
- List of Figures / List of Tables
- List of Cases/Statutes (if applicable)
- Double line spacing for most of these
- List of Plates, List of Symbols
- List of Appendices (if any)
- Optional: Abbreviations, Definitions, Glossary
- Abstract
- Then Chapter One …

11) Abstract requirements
- Max word limits:
  - depends on degree type (Thesis/Dissertation/Project) as stated in the guideline:
    - Maximum of 750/500/350 words (depending on level/type)
- Must include:
  - concise account of the project
  - objective
  - method used
  - major findings
  - major recommendations
- Notes:
  - single-spaced
  - as italics paragraph
  - avoid citations in the abstract
  - avoid abbreviations or explain at first use.

12) Chapter-level expectations (key points)
Chapter One (Introduction):
- Background of Study
- Statement of Problem:
  - addresses what/why/when/where/who/how
- Research Question
- Aim and Objectives:
  - quantifiable verbs; phrased like:
    “To design…”, “To implement…”, “To integrate…”, “To evaluate…”, etc.
- Significance of the Study
- Scope of study
- Organization of the study

Chapter Two (Literature Review):
- critical and comprehensive examination of existing research and scholarly literature
- logically arranged and up to date
- introduction to research topic, historical context
- theoretical framework (fewer theoretical frameworks; only those relevant)
- key concepts and definitions with citations
- methodologies and research designs with citations
- empirical studies with citations
- conceptual frameworks
- debates/controversies and gaps
- related works (minimum number of reviewed journals) and thematic/chronological organization

Chapter Three (System Design and Methodology / Research Methodology):
- research approach (quantitative/qualitative/mixed)
- research questions & hypotheses
- research design:
  experimental/descriptive/exploratory (as applicable)
- software development methodology (Agile/Waterfall/Scrum/DevOps) and explain adaptation to the specific goals
- data collection methods
- ethical considerations
- tools and software
- research timeline
- data presentation
- methodology for software development: include objectives, evaluation plan, development process, metrics, evaluation criteria, ethical considerations
- software/system design requirements:
  - review of methodologies (at least 4)
  - adopted/adapted methodology and why
  - system architecture/workflow
  - system modelling (at least 3 UML diagrams such as use case, activity, DFD, sequence, class, etc.)
  - system requirements (functional/non-functional; MoSCoW recommended)
  - interface design
  - database schema design

Chapter Four (Implementation and Testing / Results and Discussion):
- explain implementation (including database if used, with screenshots)
- testing:
  - demonstrate how functional + non-functional requirements are met
  - testing types to describe:
    unit/integration/performance/usability/UAT/system tests
- discuss testing environment and test data/scenarios
- results presentation and performance/system evaluation
- discussion:
  - relate results to objectives
  - compare with prior work
  - justify agreements/disagreements

Chapter Five (Summary, Conclusion and Recommendation):
- summary: highlights of major findings + how each objective was met
- conclusion: inference drawn from analysed data
- recommendations: precise, based on major findings; include areas for further research

13) Appendices
- Appendices are identified with letters (A, B, C …).
- Documents that must be included as appendices (per extracted guideline):
  - Project Proposal including original plan
  - First Progress Review Report
  - code/artefact
  - any other important validation document(s)

End of summary.
